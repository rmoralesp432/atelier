## Preserved roadmap, not silently omitted

- **Terrain and site modeling:** intentionally not started, following the user's latest direction.
- **References:** PNG/JPEG/WebP attachments now reach the multimodal API. Live interpretation awaits testing; PDF reference planes and scale calibration remain unimplemented.
- **Advanced snapping:** translation/yaw increments and live distance/angle feedback work; vertex/edge/face snapping and geometric constraints remain future work.
- **Bilingual conversation:** English/Spanish are requested by the provider prompt; live quality has not been verified.
- **Design alternatives:** undo/redo works; named alternatives and branching history do not exist yet.
- **Site analysis:** a calculated sun-position/shadow study is implemented. Annual radiation, weather, terrain and energy analysis remain future work.
- **Persistent history:** local modeling history works; cloud project storage/account synchronization remains future work.
- **Professional export:** ATELIER JSON preserves recipes/history. OBJ now exports visible triangle geometry; STL/GLB remain import-only. Native RVT/SKP are not implemented. IFC, DWG, STEP and professional CAD/BIM export remain future work.
