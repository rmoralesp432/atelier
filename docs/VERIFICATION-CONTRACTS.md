# Independent verification contracts

These are evidence contracts, not modeling recipes or fixed design briefs. The agent still constructs, inspects and repairs editable geometry using the existing tools. No extra reviewer call was added.

- **Shape:** compare evaluated occupied solids, not construction labels. A sphere has one radius; a cylinder is a right circular cylinder. Explicit ellipsoid and elliptic-cylinder predicates require measurably unequal principal/transverse radii; they denote stretched/noncircular forms and do not automatically accept spheres or circular cylinders. Search physical mesh frames to accept equivalent baked rotations. Fit failure on an unfamiliar representation is unknown, not proof of incorrect geometry. Bound the frame search and mesh size; do not claim a result when those observation limits are reached.
- **Precision:** these are polygonal solids, not analytic CAD surfaces. Round bounds/radii allow 0.5% tessellation variation and occupied-volume comparison allows 2% for curved shapes, 0.001% for boxes. They do not certify microscopic surface quality or engineering suitability. A failed sphere radial/extent invariant disproves roundness regardless of its stored recipe.
- **Dimensions:** ordinary width/depth/height and min/center/max anchors are actual world bounds. A rotated local width is not a world width. Flat profile measurements evaluate their vertices. The diameter predicate first establishes round shape, then measures its intrinsic diameter regardless of orientation. Wall/shell thickness needs unavailable surface evidence; a short bounding-box dimension does not prove it.
- **Relations:** shared solid volume establishes interior intersection/containment; `disjoint` permits boundary contact. `touching` additionally requires surface distance within tolerance and no interior overlap. Metric tolerances are cubed for volume residuals (numerical floor 1e-9 m³). An axis offset establishes alignment of bound anchors, not structural connectivity. Set quantifiers are explicit. Legacy relation means exactly two individual solids with the same physical semantics.
- **Counts/layouts:** exact integer counts; unordered one-to-one world base-center matching. A correct count alone cannot prove arrangement or alignment. Each means every selected part, not just the combined envelope.
- **Properties:** material, role and host identifiers verify assigned metadata; they are not proof of physical support, watertightness, strength or engineering performance. Group/editability checks establish distinct unlocked scene objects, not internal face editing.
- **Coverage:** the independent reader derives expected quantities and dimensional implications from the user and original scene, never from candidate dimensions. Every material clause needs a supported predicate or an explicit unavailable/qualitative outcome. Unsupported shape labels do not receive a pass from the recipe. Interpretation coverage remains a real-AI evaluation target; deterministic checks cannot prove that arbitrary natural language was interpreted completely.
- **Outcomes:** verified, incorrect and unverified remain separate. Only measured incorrect results drive repair. Unknown qualities are disclosed on safe undoable additions; unknown authorization cannot permit editing existing geometry. Identity, transaction/Undo behavior and modeling tools are unchanged.

Regression evidence includes the original real-AI channel criteria and both stretched-ball scenes, varied sizes and shapes, arbitrary rotated meshes, intrinsic diameters, contact/gaps, and existing scope/persistence tests. James's independent review and research dossier motivate individual measurements, protected-edit scope and truthfulness about unsupported evidence; their architectural examples are not implementation templates.
