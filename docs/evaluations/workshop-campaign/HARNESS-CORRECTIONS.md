## Revision 4: numerical measurement correction and explicit recovery trial

Both live W6 creations passed occupied-shape, position, count, grouping and preservation measurements. The transfer oracle nevertheless marked the highest **native box** invalid because its Float32 mesh-integrated volume differed from the kernel volume by **1.282692e-7 m³** (relative **1.571926e-7**, or 0.0000157%). Kernel topology status was `NoError`. The initial transfer-only absolute threshold of 1e-7 was inconsistent with the existing W1–W4 oracle, which uses max(1e-8, volume × 1e-5). This was an evaluator false flag, not an ATELIER false acceptance.

Revision 4 uses that existing relative-volume consistency threshold for the transfer cases, while leaving all occupied-shape, position, relationship, count, identity and unrelated-object requirements unchanged. It adds a native-box-versus-normalized-mesh calibration that reproduces this representation difference; twelve transfer controls and six recovery/native-representation controls pass. Original `transfer-1` rows, including their `COMMITTED_INCORRECT` labels, remain untouched. A separate reassessment records the corrected measurement before resuming the actual saved creations. The two resulting fixture edit trials remain separate diagnostics, not silently relabeled as edits on the created models.
