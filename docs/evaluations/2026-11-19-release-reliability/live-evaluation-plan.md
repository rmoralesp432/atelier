## Four paid tasks, plus offline controls

1. **Create a multilevel assembly through the actual application.** “Create a four-level open-frame study with a 19 m by 13 m footprint. Floor-to-floor heights are 4.2, 3.3, 3.7 and 3.3 metres, from lowest to highest. Use separate editable slabs, roof, columns and glazing panels. Make reasonable structural and façade choices. Leave the existing marker unchanged.” Seed only a distant marker and record it before generation. Independently measure the requested footprint, four occupied levels plus roof, relative floor elevations, component roles, clear-height relationships, unique identities, editability and marker preservation. Do not supply a construction recipe to the agent. Underdetermined structural spacing can be chosen by the agent; assumptions must not silently change the requested dimensions.
2. **Modify the same AI-produced assembly.** “Increase the lowest floor-to-floor height by 1.1 m. Keep the other storey heights, footprint and existing component identities. Adjust all connected elements, keeping the marker unchanged.” Compare every retained ID and its measured geometry with the first result. Check the lower slab remains fixed, upper levels translate, connected columns/glazing meet their intended levels, and unrelated geometry is exactly preserved. Select the marker and change panel/viewport layout while awaiting completion to exercise the final App commit guard. Require one Undo step for the complete edit.
3. **Detect and repair a deliberately wrong solid.** Request an independent elliptical column, 0.73 m by 0.37 m across, 0.91 m tall, rotated 37 degrees and placed at an offset location. Use the existing engine evaluation hook to replace the correctly created provisional column with a circular cross-section once, before independent verification. Record both measured states and the exact injection. The real criteria reader and modeling agent must detect the wrong section and correct it from actual verifier feedback. Preserve the object's ID. This is an injected fault, not a claim that the AI naturally made that mistake. If the prerequisite correct provisional column is never produced, report the repair test as unverified. Probe the final criteria offline with a correct equivalent mesh/axis representation, a wrong equal-span solid, and an altered protected marker; do not conflate unverified equivalence with verified acceptance.
4. **Continue after failure, reload and Undo/Redo.** First induce an offline blocked modeling turn after a provisional step in the browser. Require no geometry, context or history commit, and retain the failed-turn record. Reload, then ask the real AI: “Create a freestanding open-top tray, 1.7 m long, 0.95 m wide and 0.24 m high, with 0.04 m wall and base thickness. Keep everything else unchanged.” Independently measure its outer dimensions and probe its empty interior/base thickness; accept equivalent editable constructions. Check existing assembly/marker preservation, persistence and whole-turn Undo/Redo. Do not substitute a handmade successful assembly if tasks 1–2 fail.

Tasks 1, 2 and 4 must pass through the real App request/finalization/persistence path, not just `stagedPlan` in Node. Task 3 reuses the existing isolated fault-injection harness with the actual production engine and verifier; it must not add a fault-injection hook to the application. ## Measurements and stop rules

- Evaluate actual geometry with independent solid/bounds/intersection measurements. Names, labels, valid JSON and successful tool receipts are not sufficient. Check IDs, dimensions, elevations, relationships, editability, preservation and transaction integrity separately. Use documented small numerical/tessellation tolerances; never widen them after seeing a failure.
- Report each requested property as verified, incorrect, or unverified. Count false acceptance, false rejection, successful autonomous corrections, actual commits and post-failure recovery. A missing requirement is not a pass. Stop on unsafe acceptance, corruption, incorrect commit, or an unexplained rejection of correct geometry; preserve all evidence.
- Record raw per-call and per-turn timings, separating model execution, independent interpretation, deterministic checks, rendering/engine work and final commit. Record repairs and retry counts. Four tasks are too few for a credible general reliability percentage or p95 claim.
- Preserve original/provisional/final projects, safe request/response traces, AI-generated criteria, observations, independent measurements, browser screenshots and pre/post Undo snapshots. Never log credentials or authorization headers.
